🌱 A little note for grown-ups

Little observations. Big curiosity!

These colourful profile pages are optional companions to the Oh My Bug! Is That Really True? colouring and trivia book. Children can explore photographs and try short observation challenges, while adults can check the fuller scientific details.

  • Only real photographs will be used for the final gallery.
  • Each image will include its photographer, source and reuse licence.
  • Not every ability applies to every species — our Science Library explains the nuances.
  • No accounts, sign-ins, quizzes with personal information or subscriptions are required to browse.
